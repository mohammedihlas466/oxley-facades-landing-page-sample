01  ·  The Discipline

Façade engineering sits between architecture and structure, yet almost no degree teaches it.

A façade is the external envelope of a building — its face to the world. It governs how a building resists wind and water, holds heat, behaves in fire, admits light, and is actually assembled on site. It is an art and a science that resolves the technical challenges of delivering an architect’s intent.

Engineers arrive here from architecture, civil and mechanical backgrounds, and must re-skill because no mainstream degree treats the envelope as a discipline in its own right. Oxley Façades exists for exactly that reason.

Taught by practising engineers, not lecturers.

Cohorts are deliberately small, sessions are live rather than recorded, and the people teaching you are still resolving these details on live projects this week.

Questions

Before you apply

Do I need a construction background to start? +
No. The Certificate is designed as an entry point and assumes no prior façade experience — it is open to secondary-school leavers, graduates and anyone moving across from another discipline. If you already work in the industry, an advisor will place you at Diploma or Professional Diploma level instead.
Do I need AutoCAD or Revit already? +
No. The Certificate includes AutoCAD Essentials (CT.2) and Revit Fundamentals (CT.6), plus CAD integration and project workflow (CT.9). You finish able to draw and model façade work, not just describe it.
What do the programmes cost? +
Fees are confirmed in the prospectus issued with your enquiry, along with instalment options and any cohort concessions. Request the prospectus above and it will be sent with your placement recommendation.
When does the next cohort start? +
The Professional Diploma is currently open for applications. The Certificate and Diploma run in cohorts and are presently closed — join the waitlist and you will be notified first, before places are released publicly.
Is the qualification recognised? +
Oxley Façades is an accredited provider with The CPD Standards Office (Provider No. 22488). Completed hours are recognised for continuing professional development by professional bodies and employers.
Can I study from outside the United Kingdom? +
Yes. Teaching is live and online, and our alumni are working across seven countries. Sessions are scheduled at weekends, and the same syllabus and assessment standard apply in every region.
How much time should I set aside each week? +
Programmes run for six months in a blended, weekend format built around working professionals: live sessions plus independent study between them. Your advisor will confirm the exact schedule for your cohort before you enrol.
Do you deliver training for companies? +
Yes. Contractors, fabricators and consultancies can commission cohort training for their teams, delivered to the same accredited syllabus. Select “group or corporate training” in the enquiry form and we will send the corporate prospectus.
